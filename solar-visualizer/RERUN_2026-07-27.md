## Your 30% question — two concrete answers

1. **The reference layer state is now pinned** (it was "unknowable from your
   side"): `SOLAR_REF` (the label layer) **hidden**, everything else visible,
   **including `SITE_CONTEXT`**. Calibration above confirms this reproduces the
   shipped dataset.
2. **Likely the bigger cause: panel sampling footprint.** `raytrace.py` samples
   a 0.50 × 0.35 m rectangle (taken from the *stand-in* geometry branch of the
   `.rb`, which only triggers when no `SOLAR_LIGHT` definition exists). The
   actual `SOLAR_LIGHT` component in the committed `.skp` is a **5" × 3.5"**
   (0.127 × 0.089 m) panel — the study samples a 4× smaller (linear) footprint.
   In dappled weave shadow a big rectangle almost always clips some shadow →
   systematically lower lit fractions, and lit² + the 0.75 mismatch penalty
   compound it. Suggest setting `PANEL_W/PANEL_H = 0.127/0.089` in solarsim and
   re-validating — that plus the layer state above should close most of the gap.

Also worth knowing for exact-reproduction attempts: the shipped per-slot arrays
store *rounded* lit ints while the generator used floats internally (the ×0.75
mismatch flips off exactly at lit=1.0), so bit-exact reproduction from the JSON
is not possible — rankings and dailies are the stable quantities.
