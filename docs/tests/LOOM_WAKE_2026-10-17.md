# Loom wake investigation, October 17, 2026

Ben arrived around 13:30 PDT with nine canopies hanging beside an outdoor
stairwell and four perimeters on the bench. BenPhone was available. A
previous one-hour sleep had expired, but the T-Deck Wake app did not appear
to keep the fixtures awake.

## Initial evidence

Read-only serial capture on Ben's T-Deck 8EB508 / COM10, without a reset:
ops/bench/data/Loom/20261017T202917Z-8EB508-wake-intake.jsonl.
Only local status queries were sent, not mesh commands.

- T-Deck reports tdeck-dev-local, channel 11, zero send failures. Its exact
  deployed build cannot be established from that development revision alone.
- All 13 fixture IDs were present in its census on fx-261017-e39594d-b.
  Initial reported voltages were 3.259-3.359 V and all reported power tier 0.
  Census ages varied; this is not simultaneous fresh telemetry from all 13.
- Canopy audit records retain the 3600-second sleep from controller 979604,
  sequence 3274, alongside newer 120-second DAY_CHARGE sleeps. Thus the
  original one-hour sleep had expired; the fixtures were cycling normally.
- Tone Lab initially reported loaded=1, seven patterns, two rules, eleven
  show-roster entries, idle playback, and keep-awake off. The census has 13

- During the capture the T-Deck printed `tonelab: mode: perimeter cycle`.
  This was not sent by the capture script. Ben confirmed that Brian had
  the handheld and was operating it during the capture.

## Why the Wake app does not provide the expected hold

`app_schedule.cpp` maps Wake Fleet and Performance Hold to the legacy
NB_FORCE_LIFECYCLE mode-0 command. The bridge repeats Wake for six minutes
and Performance Hold for an hour. The fixture records that as control RX and
sets the forced lifecycle flag (or inspection control arm).

However, `behaviorTick()` returns early for both the squad-trigger and
decompression branches. Their sleep gates use the event/session state, not
the ordinary lifecycle's control-RX hold or forced-day setting. Consequently
the ordinary Wake/hold control is not an effective event-session wake hold.
It also does not change a persisted Travel event mode. Sleeping radios still
cannot receive an immediate remote command; the sender must span their next
listening window.

For this event image, Tone Lab Mode -> Perimeter cycle or Lantern dings is
the appropriate control. These modes enable the image's own awake behavior
within its power gates; Travel permits normal sleep. The current source
repeats a mode selection every two seconds for three minutes, spanning the
120-second sleep cadence. No new firmware or lantern configuration was
written by Codex during the initial investigation.

## Verification

Follow-up: ops/bench/data/Loom/20261017T203000Z-8EB508-wake-intake.jsonl.
The 160-second observer finished normally. Only local `t` and `tl-status`
queries were sent; Codex sent no radio command, reset, flash, or chime.

At 13:32:35 PDT all 13 peer reports were fresh (ages 0.243-6.148 seconds),
all reported active `life=4` and `ptier=0`, and uninterrupted current boot
uptimes were 66.129-182.170 seconds. Even the last returning canopy had
survived well beyond the former short wake window. The T-Deck's send-failure
counter remained zero; its receive count advanced 615 -> 837 during the
follow-up. This verifies awake/radio operation, not an audible show test.

Final active targets: canopies F40254, 9F2738, 9F0E54, 9F26BC, 9F26D8,
F2BE0C, 9F2638, 9F26B0, 9F0E5C; perimeters F3FD28, F401DC, F40308, F40348.
Final reported battery range was 3.243-3.358 V. The source mismatch is queued
in TODO; no new firmware was necessary for this field recovery. Future event
wake operations should use Tone Lab's Mode control rather than the legacy
Wake/Performance Hold app until those semantics are reconciled.

Separate installation observation: Sakura F2BE0C's fresh full heartbeat at
13:31:05 PDT (uptime 60.269 s, age 1.515 s) still had `sens=0 cmis=1 cls=1`.
It remains an active canopy, but its missing class sensor needs checking
before relying on its own presence detection. No sensor repair was attempted.
